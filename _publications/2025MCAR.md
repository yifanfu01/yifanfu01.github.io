I worked on this study in the Department of General Surgery at Peking Union Medical College Hospital with Dr. Yueze Liu and other colleagues, under the supervision of Prof. Taiping Zhang, Dr. Huang, and Dr. Qiu.

We compared the tumor microenvironments of pancreatic head and uncinate tumors with those of body and tail tumors using single-cell and spatial transcriptomics, bulk RNA sequencing, and patient-derived organoids. The study identified SPP1+ venous endothelial cells and found fewer CRABP2+ tumor cells in body and tail tumors than in head and uncinate tumors. These differences may help explain how tumor location relates to immune context and clinical outcomes.

We also presented this work at CAP 2025 in Chongqing, China.
