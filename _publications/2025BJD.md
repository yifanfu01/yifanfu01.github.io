I worked on this study in the Department of Dermatology at Peking Union Medical College Hospital with Dr. Chao Wu and Dr. Zhiyu Pang, under the supervision of Prof. Hongzhong Jin and Dr. Chen.

We combined spatiotemporal transcriptomics with multiplex immunofluorescence to investigate IL-36 signaling in Sweet syndrome. The findings implicated neutrophil elastase in IL-36 activation and identified interactions among neutrophils, keratinocytes, and CD8+ T cells. Two patients treated with the anti-IL-36 receptor antibody spesolimab showed favorable responses, supporting further study of this pathway in corticosteroid-resistant disease.
