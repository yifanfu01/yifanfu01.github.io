I worked on this study at Peking Union Medical College with Dr. Jinxin Tao under the supervision of Prof. Taiping Zhang and Prof. Yupei Zhao.

We integrated single-cell, spatial, and other omics data to study heterogeneity in neutrophil extracellular trap formation in pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma. The analysis linked NETosis patterns to the immune microenvironment and identified TLR2 expression as a potential prognostic marker. These associations support further investigation of neutrophil biology and TLR2-targeted strategies; they do not by themselves establish treatment efficacy.

We were invited to write about this work for *Nature Portfolio Communities*.
