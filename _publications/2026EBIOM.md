I worked on this study at Peking Union Medical College Hospital with Dr. Licheng Song and other colleagues under the supervision of Prof. Lixin Xie, Prof. Jingyu Chen, and Prof. Huaiyuan Chen.

We integrated Visium HD spatial transcriptomics with single-cell RNA sequencing to compare cellular niches across organizing pneumonia, connective tissue disease-associated interstitial lung disease, and idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis. The analysis associated a B-cell–AT2–myofibroblast niche in organizing pneumonia with glucocorticoid-responsive repair, while progressive fibrosis showed different epithelial and fibroblast states. Experiments also supported a role for B-cell-derived MIF and CD74 in fibroblast migration. We used [stGrads](https://stgrads.readthedocs.io) to examine spatial expression gradients in these tissues.

The findings also caution against automatically excluding immunoglobulin-related genes such as *JCHAIN* and *IGKC* from spatial analyses, because their expression may reflect meaningful local biology.
