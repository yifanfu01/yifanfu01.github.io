I completed this study at Beihang University with Dr. Zhirong Guo under the supervision of Dr. Shaocong Mo and Prof. Wenchao Gu.
  
We integrated single-nucleus RNA sequencing datasets to examine pathways associated with cognitive impairment in Alzheimer's disease and COVID-19. The analysis highlighted altered astrocyte and neuronal states, including pathways related to synaptic function, neuronal injury, and neuroinflammation. These findings suggest directions for further mechanistic and therapeutic research.
