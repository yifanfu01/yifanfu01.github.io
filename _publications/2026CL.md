I finished this work in the PUMCH, together with Dr. Chen, Dr. Luo and other colleagues in 2026, supervised by Prof. Taiping Zhang and Prof. Yupei Zhao.

This study focused on the mRNA m5C modification. Previous Dr. Chen et al. identified TRDMT1 is one of the m5C writer, and carried out several vital biological process (Nat Comm. 2023). Here, we explored the potential gemcitabine-resistance induced by TRDMT1. We applied BS-RNA-seq together with other wet/dry methods, and demonstrated that TRDMT1-IRS2 m5C-RAD52 axis links DNA repair and drug resistance in pancreatic cancer.
