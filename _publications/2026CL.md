I worked on this study at Peking Union Medical College Hospital with Dr. Wenhao Luo, Dr. Hao Chen, and other colleagues under the supervision of Prof. Taiping Zhang and Prof. Yupei Zhao.

We investigated how TRDMT1-mediated mRNA m5C methylation contributes to chemotherapy resistance in pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma. Sequencing and functional experiments linked TRDMT1 to IRS2 expression and the recruitment of homologous recombination proteins, including RAD52. These findings suggest that TRDMT1 supports DNA repair and reduces sensitivity to several chemotherapeutic agents.
