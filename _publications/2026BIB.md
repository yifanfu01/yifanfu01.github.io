I developed this work with Dr. Fan Zhang of Beihang University and other colleagues under the supervision of Prof. Taiping Zhang and Prof. Wei Chen.

In this study, we introduced [stGrads](https://stgrads.readthedocs.io), a computational framework for spot-based and bin-based spatial transcriptomics data. It models how gene expression and cell composition change with distance from a defined cell population or tissue region, and identifies distance-responsive genes. Applications across multiple datasets demonstrated its use in characterizing spatial interactions in healthy and diseased tissues.
