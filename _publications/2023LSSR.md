I contributed to this study at Beihang University's Lunar Palace 1 laboratory with Dr. Yueying Lu and fellow students under the supervision of Prof. Yuming Fu.
  
We examined how simulated microgravity and low-dose ionizing radiation, separately and together, affected a synthetic bacterial community derived from a spacecraft assembly room. The community showed increased diversity and changes in composition under simulated space conditions; low-dose radiation was also associated with increased penicillin resistance. These findings inform future studies of microbial risks in spacecraft environments.
  
The results may help guide microbial monitoring and astronaut health protection during long-duration missions.
