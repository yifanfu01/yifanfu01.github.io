I developed this review at Peking Union Medical College Hospital with my coauthors under the supervision of Prof. Taiping Zhang and Prof. Yupei Zhao.
  
We synthesized evidence from single-cell sequencing, spatial omics, and related technologies to describe cellular diversity in the pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma microenvironment. The review discusses cell-type markers, functional states, and computational approaches, with particular attention to myeloid cells and cancer-associated fibroblasts.

We also highlight underexplored populations, including neutrophils and endocrine cells, as priorities for future work.

The review was featured by [iNature](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/TKL3gUtBSAyhjSxpphMZIA).
