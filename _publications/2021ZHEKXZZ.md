I completed this review at Beihang University under the supervision of Prof. Jing Ji.
  
We reviewed the challenges of studying Eustachian tube dysfunction and compared the strengths and limitations of animal, cadaveric, and computational models. The article discusses how numerical simulations could complement experimental models and inform future research.
  
I remain interested in improving finite element models of the Eustachian tube and exploring their potential clinical applications.
