I am an M.D. candidate in the [4+4 Medical Doctor Program](https://mdadmission.pumc.edu.cn/mdweb/site!index) at Peking Union Medical College (PUMC) and the Chinese Academy of Medical Sciences (CAMS). I earned bachelor's degrees in biomedical engineering and mathematics and applied mathematics from Beihang University in 2023, and received the President's Award. I am grateful to the mentors and colleagues who have guided my work. You can read more about my experience in my [CV](/cv/).
{: .text-justify }

My research focuses on computational methods for clinical and basic biomedical research. I am particularly interested in integrating single-cell and spatial multiomics data to study the tissue microenvironment in pancreatic cancer, inflammatory diseases, and lung fibrosis.
{: .text-justify }

With my colleagues, I have used high-resolution spatial transcriptomics to study neutrophil–IL-36 signaling in Sweet syndrome and the cellular niches associated with different forms of lung fibrosis. We also developed *stGrads*, a computational framework for analyzing spatial expression gradients and identifying distance-responsive genes. In related biomechanics projects, I have helped develop and evaluate finite element analysis (FEA) models of the spine and zygoma.
{: .text-justify }

I hope to become a physician who combines strong clinical skills, rigorous research, and ethical practice.
{: .text-justify }

I have served as a reviewer for journals including *Apoptosis*, *Clinical and Translational Medicine*, *BMC Bioinformatics*, *Frontiers in Genetics*, *Frontiers in Immunology*, *Frontiers in Neurology*, *Scientific Reports*, *Journal of Surgical Case Reports*, and *Archives of Gynecology and Obstetrics*.
{: .text-justify }
