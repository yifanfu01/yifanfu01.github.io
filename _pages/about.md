I am an M.D. candidate in the [4+4 Medical Doctor Program](https://mdadmission.pumc.edu.cn/mdweb/site!index) at Peking Union Medical College (PUMC) and the Chinese Academy of Medical Sciences (CAMS). Before entering medicine, I earned bachelor's degrees in biomedical engineering and mathematics and applied mathematics from Beihang University in 2023, where I received the President's Award. You can read more about my training and research in my [CV](/cv/).
{: .text-justify }

My research is motivated by clinical questions and focuses on using computational biology to investigate fundamental disease mechanisms, particularly in pancreatic cancer. I am interested in how cellular states and interactions shape the pancreatic tumor microenvironment, and how these patterns relate to disease progression and treatment response. I use single-cell and spatial multiomics data to approach these questions.
{: .text-justify }

In pancreatic cancer, I have worked with colleagues on neutrophil heterogeneity, differences in the tumor microenvironment across anatomical sites, and mechanisms of treatment resistance. Related collaborations have taken me into Sweet syndrome and lung fibrosis, where spatial transcriptomics can help connect cellular behavior with tissue context. We also developed *stGrads*, a computational framework for analyzing spatial expression gradients. My earlier work in biomechanics helped shape this interdisciplinary approach.
{: .text-justify }

I hope to grow into a physician-scientist who brings careful clinical observation together with rigorous quantitative research and ethical care. I am grateful to the mentors, colleagues, and friends who continue to guide and challenge me.
{: .text-justify }

I have also served as a reviewer for journals including *Apoptosis*, *Clinical and Translational Medicine*, *BMC Bioinformatics*, *Frontiers in Genetics*, *Frontiers in Immunology*, *Frontiers in Neurology*, *Scientific Reports*, *Journal of Surgical Case Reports*, and *Archives of Gynecology and Obstetrics*.
{: .text-justify }
